# Audio Quality v4 roadmap

## Phase 1 — measurable audition chain

- Deterministic before/after render path.
- Objective receipts for RMS, peak, crest, DC and stereo side/mid energy.
- No production mutation.
- CI smoke proving deterministic, bounded output.

## Phase 2 — flagship blind listening

Rooms: rooftop, window, headspace, last-bus.

For each room:

- Render 3–4 long-form seeds at 48–64 bars.
- Compare untreated vs v4-finished candidates blind.
- Record Music Lab scores and comments.
- Reject candidates that sound synthetic, fatiguing, repetitive or poorly matched to the room even when metrics pass.

## Phase 3 — source-quality decision

If finishing improves polish but does not make the compositions compelling, do not keep stacking DSP. Move the flagship rooms to the existing studio-master intake using original commissioned/human-produced or appropriately licensed first-party-cleared masters, or a separately governed Open Music Studio generation source with explicit commercial-use provenance.

## Phase 4 — release qualification

Only approved exact files proceed through mastering certification, production review, release certificate and hosted device UAT.

## Phase 5 — scale

Only after flagship rooms are clearly better should the process expand to the remaining catalog. Visual, social and Broadcast work stays secondary to this gate.
