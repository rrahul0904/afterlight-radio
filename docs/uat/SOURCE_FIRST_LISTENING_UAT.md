# Afterlight source-first listening UAT

Run this only against an exact-head Preview/candidate. The goal is to test whether the place-based listening product works **before** radio/AI-host expansion.

## Participants

Five people who did not build the product. Use ordinary listeners, not only developers.

## Devices

Across the five sessions include:
- phone speaker;
- headphones/earbuds;
- laptop speaker.

## Task

Give only this instruction:

> Pick somewhere you want to be, start listening, and use it the way you naturally would while reading, working, or relaxing.

Do not explain rooms, `Another view`, ambience, or sharing unless the participant becomes completely blocked.

## Minimum listening evidence

For each flagship room promoted as a baseline:
- at least two human reviews;
- at least 15 minutes of listening per reviewer;
- record source/provider and exact catalog revision.

## Observe

Record:
- which room is selected first and why;
- time until playback starts;
- unexpected silence or provider failure;
- whether room music feels distinct from the other rooms;
- when repetition becomes noticeable;
- whether `Another view` is understood as visual-only;
- whether visual changes ever interrupt playback;
- whether ambience helps or distracts;
- whether controls disappear into the background enough for long sessions;
- whether the listener voluntarily keeps the page open.

## Ratings (1–5)

For each tested room:
- music quality;
- room fit;
- low-distraction usefulness;
- fatigue after 15 minutes;
- catalog variety/repetition;
- visual atmosphere;
- willingness to use again.

## Exit questions

1. Which room would you return to?
2. Would you leave this playing while doing something else?
3. What made the room feel like a place rather than a playlist?
4. Did any control or visual element break the mood?
5. Did the music ever feel repetitive or low quality?
6. What would make you open this instead of YouTube/Spotify/a normal playlist?

## Gate before radio becomes critical path

- at least 4/5 understand scene selection without instruction;
- at least 4/5 find a room they would voluntarily keep playing;
- no unexplained playback stop in the tested path;
- visual switching never changes/restarts music;
- human reviewers rate the promoted music source acceptable on all three device classes;
- provider/legal boundary is satisfied for every promoted catalog entry.

A waveform/mastering score is not a substitute for listening evidence.
