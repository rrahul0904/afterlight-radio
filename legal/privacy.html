Afterlight

Privacy, without the fog.

Afterlight is designed to collect very little. We do not run advertising trackers or sell personal data.

Account data

If you create an account, we store your email address, listening preferences, saved places and subscription entitlement so your experience can follow you across devices. If you continue with Google, Google supplies the basic account identity information required to sign you in; Afterlight does not receive your Google password.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Afterlight does not receive or store full card numbers. We retain Stripe customer and subscription identifiers needed to unlock Afterlight+ and support billing.

Product analytics

We record limited first-party events such as page views, room plays, upgrade views, timer use and application errors. These events help us understand product quality. We do not use them for cross-site advertising.

Data choices

You may sign out at any time. Account deletion, data export and privacy requests are handled through support.

Processors

Our core service providers are Vercel for web hosting and delivery, Neon for authentication, application data and server-side functions, Google when you choose Google OAuth for account authentication, and Stripe for payment and subscription processing.
