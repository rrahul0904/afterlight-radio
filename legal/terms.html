The service

Afterlight provides illustrated listening environments, original audio and related features. The free collection may be used without a paid subscription. Afterlight+ unlocks premium rooms and subscriber features while the subscription remains entitled.

Subscriptions

Paid plans renew according to the billing period shown at checkout until cancelled. When Stripe self-service billing is available, eligible subscribers can manage billing from the Afterlight Account area. When self-service billing is unavailable, billing changes and cancellation requests are handled through Afterlight Support. We use the subscription account information to process the request. Unless required by law, completed billing periods are generally non-refundable.
