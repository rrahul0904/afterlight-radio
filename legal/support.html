Signed-in subscribers can open Account → Manage subscription to update payment details or cancel through Stripe's secure customer portal.

Playback

If audio does not start, tap Play once while the browser is active, confirm the device is not muted, and check the Afterlight volume control. iPhone and Safari require a user gesture before starting audio.

Account links

Sign-in links are one-time links and expire. If one has expired, request a new link from the Account dialog.
