Tell us what happened. Support requests are stored securely with the product so they can be reviewed and matched to your Afterlight account when needed.

Billing

Afterlight+ checkout is handled securely by Stripe. Billing changes and subscription cancellations are handled through this support form when self-service billing is not available. Choose Billing or Subscription cancellation; your request is saved in Afterlight and can be matched to your subscription account.

Playback

If audio does not start, tap Play once while the browser is active, confirm the device is not muted, and check the Afterlight volume control. iPhone and Safari require a user gesture before starting audio.

Account access

Use the Account area to create an account or sign in. Your saved rooms and listening preferences sync after authentication.
